jealousy

to put it plainly and completely unpoetically, i feel jealous. a good preface is that i have no right to be jealous anymore (not like i had much right while we were together, but much less so now). we aren't together and so who you talk to, who you hangout with, who you share intimate moments with is none of my business. yet i'm boiling over with jealousy imagining who you might be with and what you might be doing. i'll admit it is ever so slightly easier to manage being jealous since we aren't together, but only because i'm reminded that i have no right to be. i saw that you were driving around and all i could think was "is she with another guy?" "are they listening to the same songs we did while we drove?" "am i opening myself up only to be hurt again?"

i hate these feelings so much. i've been checking your website every 15 minutes to see if you've updated it and nothing. the reasonable explanation: you're busy. where my mind goes to: you've grown bored of me again. i wake up every day scared to see if you've deleted everything of us. i'm waiting for the day you stop checking my website. am i just waiting for my fears to be confirmed? what makes that question worse is that my fears were confirmed. maybe not exactly how they existed in my mind, but none the less, my fears were real. you asked me once if i think my jealousy is a problem in relationships and i answered "100% yes." i have no control over it as shameful as that is to admit.

i am obsessive and possessive.

i remember you telling me that you were okay with me being obsessive, but i don't think anyone would be okay with that if they understood what it involves nor do i think it's healthy at all. i'm incapable of loving you casually or "healthily". i want the idea of being with another person to make you sick and ridden with disgust like it does with me. i want to never doubt that your eyes are fixed only on me. i want every drop of you to myself. i want to give every drop of myself to you.

but these things are impossible to ask of another person. and i know that.

i want simpler things too: i want you to like my family, i want my family to like you, i want to like your family, i want your family to like me, i want my friends to like you, i want your friends to like me, i want to trust your choices and i want you to trust mine.

i'm so scared all the time.

for now i'll keep thinking of denny's and allow myself to be excited.
